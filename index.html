Our Story

Born from a passion
for perfection

Relief was born from a simple idea: creating a place where people can slow down, relax, and enjoy exceptional coffee and handcrafted desserts. Every cup is prepared with passion, every detail designed to create comfort. We source our beans from the finest farms in Ethiopia and Colombia, roasted to perfection to deliver that unforgettable cup that becomes your daily ritual.

2020
The Beginning
Relief opened its first location with a simple dream — to elevate everyday coffee into an art form, one cup at a time.
2022
The Dessert Chapter
Introduced our signature cheesecake line and handcrafted waffles, transforming Relief into the city's most beloved dessert destination.
2023
Signature Collection
Launched our exclusive Signature Drinks menu — cold brews, specialty pours, and seasonal creations that became instant icons.
2024
Recognition & Community
Voted Best Specialty Cafe by over 15,000 guests. What began as a vision became a family — and we're just getting started.
